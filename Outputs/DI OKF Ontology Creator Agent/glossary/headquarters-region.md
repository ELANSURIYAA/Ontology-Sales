# Headquarters Region

## Business Definition

Global region where the customer headquarters is located.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_customer  
**Source Column**: hq_region  
**Entity**: Customer Dimension  
**Attribute**: Headquarters Region

---

## Business Meaning

Headquarters Region identifies the global region location of the customer's corporate headquarters. This attribute enables high-level geographic customer analysis and supports regional customer distribution reporting.
