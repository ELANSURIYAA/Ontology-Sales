# Glossary Index

## Overview

This index contains all business glossary terms and concepts defined in the semantic model.

---

## Business Concepts

### [Booking Transaction](booking-transaction.md)
Individual completed sales booking event representing a customer purchase.

---

### [Annual Contract Value](annual-contract-value.md)
Annualized revenue value of a contract or subscription.

---

### [Total Contract Value](total-contract-value.md)
Total revenue value of a contract over its full term.

---

### [Renewal](renewal.md)
Continuation or extension of an existing customer contract or subscription.

---

### [Net New Business](net-new-business.md)
Revenue from new customer acquisitions or expansion sales to existing customers.

---

### [Customer Segment](customer-segment.md)
Market segment classification of customer organizations.

---

### [Product Family](product-family.md)
Higher-level grouping of related products within the portfolio.

---

### [Technology Domain](technology-domain.md)
Technology area or solution domain that products belong to.

---

### [Route to Market](route-to-market.md)
Sales channel path through which products or services are sold to customers.

---

### [Fiscal Period](fiscal-period.md)
Financial reporting period used for sales and revenue analysis.
