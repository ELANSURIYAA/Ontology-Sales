# Month Name

## Business Definition

Name of the calendar month for the date.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date  
**Source Column**: month_name  
**Entity**: Date Dimension  
**Attribute**: Month Name

---

## Business Meaning

Month Name provides the descriptive name of the calendar month (e.g., January, February) for reporting and analysis. It enables month-level aggregation and month-based trend analysis.
