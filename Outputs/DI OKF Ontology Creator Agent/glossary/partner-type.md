# Partner Type

## Business Definition

Classification of the partner, such as distributor, reseller, systems integrator, or direct channel. This categorizes partners by their role in the sales channel.

---

## Business Meaning

Partner Type classifies partners by their role and function in the sales and distribution channel. Common partner types include Distributor (wholesale distribution), Reseller (retail sales partner), Systems Integrator (solution implementation partner), Managed Service Provider, and Direct Channel (company-owned sales). This classification enables channel strategy analysis and partner ecosystem management.
