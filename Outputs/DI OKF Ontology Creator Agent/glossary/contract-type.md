# Contract Type

## Business Definition

Type of commercial agreement or support entitlement, such as Enterprise Agreement, SaaS Subscription, or Solution Support. This classifies contracts by their commercial structure.

---

## Business Meaning

Contract Type represents the classification of the commercial agreement or support entitlement associated with a booking. Common contract types include Enterprise Agreement (comprehensive multi-product agreement), SaaS Subscription (cloud software subscription), Software Subscription (on-premise software subscription), Solution Support (technical support contract), and Professional Services Agreement. This classification enables contract portfolio analysis and commercial model tracking.
