# Contract Type

## Business Definition

Type of commercial agreement associated with the booking, such as Enterprise Agreement, SaaS Subscription, or support contract.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_contract  
**Source Column**: contract_type  
**Entity**: Contract Dimension  
**Attribute**: Contract Type

---

## Business Meaning

Contract Type classifies the nature of the commercial agreement under which the booking was made. This classification enables analysis of booking performance by agreement type and supports contract portfolio management.
