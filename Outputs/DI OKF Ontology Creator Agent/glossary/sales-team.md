# Sales Team

## Business Definition

Team or organizational unit the sales representative belongs to. This identifies the sales team or organizational grouping for the sales representative.

---

## Business Meaning

Sales Team represents the team or organizational unit that the sales representative belongs to for management and reporting purposes. Teams may be organized by geography, customer segment, product line, or other criteria. Examples include "Enterprise Sales Team", "Commercial Sales Team", "West Region Team", or "Cloud Sales Team". This classification enables team-based performance tracking and organizational analysis.
