# Sales Team

## Business Definition

Sales team or organization to which the representative belongs.

---

## Technical Mapping

## Business Meaning

Sales Team identifies the organizational team or group to which the sales representative belongs, enabling team-level performance tracking and supporting team-based management and incentive programs.
