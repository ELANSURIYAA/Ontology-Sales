# Fiscal Period Sequence

## Business Definition

Sequential number representing the fiscal period order in the reporting calendar.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date  
**Source Column**: fiscal_period_seq  
**Entity**: Date Dimension  
**Attribute**: Fiscal Period Sequence

---

## Business Meaning

Fiscal Period Sequence provides a sequential numbering of fiscal periods enabling period-over-period calculations, trending analysis, and chronological ordering of fiscal periods.
