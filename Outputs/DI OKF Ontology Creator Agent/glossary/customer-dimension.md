# Customer Dimension

## Business Definition

Stores descriptive customer attributes used to analyze bookings by customer identity, segment, industry, account tier, and headquarters location.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_customer  
**Entity**: Customer Dimension  
**Attribute**: N/A (Entity-level term)

---

## Business Meaning

The Customer Dimension provides customer master data for analyzing booking transactions. It enables customer-centric analysis by segment, industry, account tier, and geographic location of customer headquarters.
