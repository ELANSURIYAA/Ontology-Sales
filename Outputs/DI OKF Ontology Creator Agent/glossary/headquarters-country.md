# Headquarters Country

## Business Definition

Country where the customer organization's headquarters is located.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_customer  
**Source Column**: hq_country  
**Entity**: Customer Dimension  
**Attribute**: Headquarters Country

---

## Business Meaning

Headquarters Country identifies the country location of the customer's corporate headquarters. This attribute enables geographic customer analysis and supports regional customer distribution reporting.
