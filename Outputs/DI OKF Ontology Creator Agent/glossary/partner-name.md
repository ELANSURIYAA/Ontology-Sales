# Partner Name

## Business Definition

Name of the distributor, reseller, integrator, or direct channel partner involved in the booking.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_partner  
**Source Column**: partner_name  
**Entity**: Partner Dimension  
**Attribute**: Partner Name

---

## Business Meaning

Partner Name is the descriptive name of the partner organization used for reporting, analysis, and business communication. It provides human-readable identification of partners in reports and dashboards.
