# Technology Domain

## Business Definition

Technology area to which the product belongs, such as networking, security, or collaboration.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_product  
**Source Column**: technology_domain  
**Entity**: Product Dimension

---

## Business Meaning

Technology Domain classifies products by their technology area, enabling technology-specific analysis and supporting technology investment decisions and portfolio strategy.
