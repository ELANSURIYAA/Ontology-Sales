# Sales Representative Dimension

## Business Definition

Stores sales representative attributes used to analyze bookings by sales person, role, team, and market segment coverage.

## Business Meaning

The Sales Representative Dimension provides sales organization context for booking transactions. It enables sales performance management through sales representative attributes including role, team, and segment coverage.
