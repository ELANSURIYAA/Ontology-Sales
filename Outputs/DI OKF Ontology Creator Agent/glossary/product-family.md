# Product Family

## Business Definition

Higher-level grouping of related products within the portfolio.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_product  
**Source Column**: product_family  
**Entity**: Product Dimension

## Business Meaning

Product Family groups related products together for portfolio analysis and reporting. This hierarchical classification enables family-level performance tracking and supports product portfolio management.
