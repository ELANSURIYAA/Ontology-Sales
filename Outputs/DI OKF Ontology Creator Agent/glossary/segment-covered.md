# Segment Covered

## Business Definition

Customer segment or market segment covered by the sales representative. This identifies the customer segment or market focus assigned to the sales representative.

---

## Business Meaning

Segment Covered represents the customer segment or market segment that the sales representative is assigned to cover. This alignment ensures that sales representatives focus on specific customer types or market segments. Examples include "Enterprise", "Commercial", "Public Sector", or "Service Provider". This classification enables segment-based performance tracking and territory alignment analysis.
