# Fiscal Year

## Business Definition

Fiscal year used for business reporting and financial analysis.

## Business Meaning

Fiscal Year represents the organization's fiscal year for financial reporting and planning. The fiscal year may differ from the calendar year depending on the organization's fiscal calendar structure.
