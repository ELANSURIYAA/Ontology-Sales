# Fiscal Year

## Business Definition

Fiscal year used for financial and sales reporting. This represents the organization's fiscal year which may differ from the calendar year.

---

## Business Meaning

Fiscal Year represents the organization's fiscal year used for financial and sales reporting. Fiscal years are defined by the organization and may align with the calendar year (January-December) or follow a different cycle (e.g., July-June, October-September). Fiscal Year is the primary time dimension for internal performance reporting, quota management, and financial planning.
