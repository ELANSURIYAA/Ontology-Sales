# Calendar Year

## Business Definition

Four-digit calendar year of the date.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date

## Business Meaning

Calendar Year represents the standard four-digit year (e.g., 2024, 2025) for calendar-based reporting and analysis. It enables year-level aggregation and year-over-year comparisons.
