# Calendar Year

## Business Definition

Calendar year associated with the date. This represents the standard calendar year (January 1 to December 31) for the date.

---

## Business Meaning

Calendar Year represents the standard calendar year (e.g., 2023, 2024) associated with a date. Calendar years run from January 1 through December 31 and are used for calendar-based reporting and year-over-year comparisons. Calendar Year may differ from Fiscal Year depending on the organization's fiscal calendar.
