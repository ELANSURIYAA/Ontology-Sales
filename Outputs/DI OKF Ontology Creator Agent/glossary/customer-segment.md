# Customer Segment

## Business Definition

Market segment to which the customer belongs, such as Enterprise, Service Provider, or Public Sector.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_customer  
**Source Column**: segment  
**Entity**: Customer Dimension  
**Attribute**: Customer Segment

---

## Business Meaning

Customer Segment classifies customers into market segments based on size, business model, and strategic importance. This segmentation enables targeted analysis, segment-specific strategies, and performance benchmarking across customer types.
