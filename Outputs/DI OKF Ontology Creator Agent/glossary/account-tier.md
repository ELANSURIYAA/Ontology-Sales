# Account Tier

## Business Definition

Strategic importance or service tier assigned to the customer account. This classification indicates the priority level and service commitment for the customer.

---

## Business Meaning

Account Tier represents the strategic importance or service level assigned to a customer account. Common tiers include Strategic, Enterprise, Corporate, and Standard. Higher tiers typically indicate larger revenue potential, strategic partnerships, or premium service commitments. Account tier influences resource allocation, service levels, and account management strategies.
