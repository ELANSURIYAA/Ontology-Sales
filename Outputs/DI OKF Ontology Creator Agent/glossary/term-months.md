# Term Months

## Business Definition

Number of months covered by the contract or subscription term. This represents the duration of the contractual commitment.

---

## Business Meaning

Term Months represents the length of the contract or subscription term expressed in months. Common term lengths include 12 months (1 year), 24 months (2 years), 36 months (3 years), and 60 months (5 years). This attribute is used to calculate annualized metrics (ACV) from total contract values (TCV) and to analyze contract duration preferences and renewal timing.

---
