# Business Entity

## Business Definition

Internal business portfolio or organizational unit associated with the product. This identifies which internal business unit or portfolio owns the product.

---

## Business Meaning

Business Entity represents the internal organizational unit, business portfolio, or division responsible for the product. This classification enables internal performance tracking, portfolio management, and organizational accountability. Examples might include "Enterprise Networking Division", "Security Business Unit", or "Cloud Services Group".
