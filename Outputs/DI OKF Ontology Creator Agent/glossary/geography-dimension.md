# Geography Dimension

## Business Definition

Stores geographic attributes used to analyze bookings by region, theater, and country.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_geography  
**Entity**: Geography Dimension  
**Attribute**: N/A (Entity-level term)

## Business Meaning

The Geography Dimension provides geographic context for booking transactions. It enables regional sales analytics through hierarchical geographic attributes including region, theater, and country.
