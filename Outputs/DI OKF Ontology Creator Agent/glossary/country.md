# Country

## Business Definition

Country associated with the geography record. This represents the most granular level of geographic classification for sales territory.

---

## Business Meaning

Country represents the specific country associated with a geographic record in the sales territory hierarchy. This is the most detailed level of geographic classification, enabling country-specific analysis and reporting. Country sits at the bottom of the geographic hierarchy below Region and Theater.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Field**: geographies.country  
**Data Type**: String/Category  
**Dimension**: Yes
