# Sales Representative ID

## Business Definition

Business identifier assigned to the sales representative.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_sales_rep  
**Source Column**: rep_id  
**Entity**: Sales Representative Dimension  
**Attribute**: Sales Representative ID

---

## Business Meaning

Sales Representative ID is the business key used to identify sales representatives in operational systems. Unlike the Sales Representative Key (surrogate key), the Sales Representative ID is a meaningful business identifier that may be visible to users.

---

## Synonyms

- Rep ID
- Employee ID
- Sales Rep Number
