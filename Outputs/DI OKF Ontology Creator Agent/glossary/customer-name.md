# Customer Name

## Business Definition

Name of the customer organization that placed the order.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_customer  
**Source Column**: customer_name  
**Entity**: Customer Dimension  
**Attribute**: Customer Name

---

## Business Meaning

Customer Name is the descriptive name of the customer organization used for reporting, analysis, and business communication. It provides human-readable identification of customers in reports and dashboards.
