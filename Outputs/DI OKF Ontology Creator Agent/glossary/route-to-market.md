# Route to Market

## Business Definition

Sales route through which the booking was transacted, such as direct, reseller, or two-tier.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_partner  
**Source Column**: route_to_market  
**Entity**: Partner Dimension  
**Attribute**: Route to Market

---

## Business Meaning

Route to Market identifies the sales channel path used to transact the booking, enabling analysis of channel effectiveness and supporting channel strategy optimization.
