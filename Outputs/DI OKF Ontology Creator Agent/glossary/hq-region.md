# Headquarters Region

## Business Definition

Broad geographic region of the customer organization's headquarters. This represents the high-level geographic area where the customer's main office is located.

---

## Business Meaning

Headquarters Region represents the broad geographic region where the customer organization's headquarters is located. Common regions include Americas, EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa), APAC (Asia Pacific), and others. This high-level geographic classification enables regional customer analysis and market distribution tracking.
