# Theater

## Business Definition

Intermediate sales territory grouping within a region. This represents a mid-level geographic classification between region and country.

---

## Business Meaning

Theater represents an intermediate level of geographic territory classification that sits between Region and Country in the geographic hierarchy. Theaters group multiple countries within a region for operational management and sales territory organization. Examples might include "North America", "Southern Europe", "Southeast Asia", or "Middle East". This classification enables theater-level performance tracking and territory management.
