# Partner Dimension

## Business Definition

Stores channel partner attributes used to analyze bookings by partner identity, partner type, partner tier, and route to market.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_partner  
**Entity**: Partner Dimension  
**Attribute**: N/A (Entity-level term)

---

## Business Meaning

The Partner Dimension provides channel partner context for booking transactions. It enables channel sales analytics through partner attributes including partner type, tier, and route to market.
