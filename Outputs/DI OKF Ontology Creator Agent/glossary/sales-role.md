# Sales Role

## Business Definition

Job role or selling responsibility of the sales representative. This identifies the specific sales function or position held by the sales representative.

---

## Business Meaning

Sales Role represents the job role or selling responsibility of the sales representative. Common sales roles include Account Executive (primary customer relationship owner), Sales Engineer (technical pre-sales support), Inside Sales Representative (remote/phone sales), Field Sales Representative (territory-based sales), and Sales Manager (team leadership). This classification enables role-based performance analysis and resource planning.
