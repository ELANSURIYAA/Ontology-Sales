# Product ID

## Business Definition

Business identifier or SKU assigned to the product or subscription offer.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_product  
**Source Column**: product_id  
**Entity**: Product Dimension  
**Attribute**: Product ID

---

## Business Meaning

Product ID is the business key or SKU used to identify products in operational systems. Unlike the Product Key (surrogate key), the Product ID is a meaningful business identifier that may be visible to users and customers.

---

## Synonyms

- SKU
- Product Code
- Item Number
