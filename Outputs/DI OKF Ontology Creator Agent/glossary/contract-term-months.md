# Contract Term Months

## Business Definition

Number of months covered by the contract or service agreement.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_contract  
**Source Column**: term_months  
**Entity**: Contract Dimension  
**Attribute**: Contract Term Months

---

## Business Meaning

Contract Term Months specifies the duration of the contract in months. This attribute is used to analyze contract length distribution, calculate annualized values, and support contract renewal planning.
