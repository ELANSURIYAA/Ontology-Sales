# Unit List Price USD

## Business Definition

Standard list price per unit in US dollars before discounts. This represents the published price for a single unit of the product or service.

---

## Business Meaning

Unit List Price USD is the standard published price per unit before any discounts or pricing adjustments are applied. It serves as the baseline for calculating booking amounts and analyzing discount effectiveness. The actual realized price is calculated by applying the discount percentage to the list price.
