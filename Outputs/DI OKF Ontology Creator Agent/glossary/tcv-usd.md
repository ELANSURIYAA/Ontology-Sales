# TCV USD

## Business Definition

Total contract value of the booking over the full contract term in US dollars. This represents the complete value commitment over the entire contract duration.

---

## Business Meaning

TCV USD (Total Contract Value) represents the full contract value over the entire contract term. Unlike ACV which normalizes to an annual basis, TCV captures the complete value commitment. For a 3-year contract worth $300K, the TCV would be $300K while the ACV would be $100K. This metric is important for understanding total deal size and long-term revenue commitments.
