# Offer Type

## Business Definition

Commercial offer classification, such as hardware, software subscription, or SaaS subscription. This categorizes products by their commercial delivery model.

---

## Business Meaning

Offer Type classifies products by their commercial delivery and licensing model. Common offer types include Hardware (physical equipment), Software Subscription (on-premise software with subscription licensing), SaaS Subscription (cloud-hosted software as a service), Perpetual License, Professional Services, and Support Services. This classification enables business model analysis and revenue stream tracking.
