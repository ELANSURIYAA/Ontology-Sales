# Offer Type

## Business Definition

Commercial offer classification, such as hardware, SaaS subscription, or software subscription.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_product  
**Source Column**: offer_type  
**Entity**: Product Dimension  
**Attribute**: Offer Type

---

## Business Meaning

Offer Type classifies the commercial nature of the product offering, enabling analysis of offer mix, subscription trends, and revenue composition by offer category.
