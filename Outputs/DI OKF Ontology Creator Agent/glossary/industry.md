# Industry

## Business Definition

Industry classification of the customer organization. This categorizes customers by their primary industry vertical or business sector.

---

## Business Meaning

Industry represents the primary business sector or vertical market in which the customer organization operates. Examples include Financial Services, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, Technology, and Telecommunications. Industry classification enables vertical-specific analysis, market penetration tracking, and industry-focused sales strategies.
