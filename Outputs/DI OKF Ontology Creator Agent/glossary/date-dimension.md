# Date Dimension

## Business Definition

Stores calendar and fiscal date attributes used to analyze bookings across time periods, fiscal years, quarters, and months.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date  
**Entity**: Date Dimension  
**Attribute**: N/A (Entity-level term)

---

## Business Meaning

The Date Dimension provides temporal context for booking transactions. It enables time-based analysis across calendar and fiscal periods, supporting trend analysis, fiscal reporting, and period-over-period comparisons.
