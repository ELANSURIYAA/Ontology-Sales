# Partner Tier

## Business Definition

Program tier or status level assigned to the partner. This represents the partner's status within the partner program based on performance and capabilities.

---

## Business Meaning

Partner Tier represents the status level or tier assigned to a partner within the partner program. Common tiers include Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Authorized, typically based on sales performance, technical capabilities, certifications, and strategic alignment. Higher tiers often receive better margins, support, and benefits. This classification enables partner program management and performance tracking.
