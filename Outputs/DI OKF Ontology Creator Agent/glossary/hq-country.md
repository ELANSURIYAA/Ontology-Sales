# Headquarters Country

## Business Definition

Country where the customer organization's headquarters is located. This identifies the primary country location of the customer's main office.

---

## Business Meaning

Headquarters Country represents the country where the customer organization's main headquarters or principal office is located. This geographic attribute is used for customer location analysis, regional market penetration, and geographic revenue distribution. It differs from the sales geography which represents where the sale occurred.
