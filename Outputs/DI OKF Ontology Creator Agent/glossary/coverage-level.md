# Coverage Level

## Business Definition

Service or support coverage level provided under the contract. This defines the scope and intensity of support services included in the contract.

---

## Business Meaning

Coverage Level represents the service or support coverage level provided under the contract. Common coverage levels include Basic (standard business hours support), Enhanced (extended hours support), Premium (24x7 support with faster response times), and Mission Critical (highest priority support with dedicated resources). This classification enables support portfolio analysis and service level tracking.
