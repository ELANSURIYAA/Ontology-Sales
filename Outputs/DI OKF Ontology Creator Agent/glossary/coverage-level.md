# Coverage Level

## Business Definition

Service or support coverage level provided under the contract.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_contract  
**Source Column**: coverage_level  
**Entity**: Contract Dimension  
**Attribute**: Coverage Level

---

## Business Meaning

Coverage Level specifies the tier or level of service and support provided under the contract. This attribute enables analysis of service mix, premium versus standard coverage adoption, and support revenue contribution.
