# Booking Type

## Business Definition

Classification of the booking event, such as new business or renewal.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.fact_bookings  
**Source Column**: booking_type  
**Entity**: Booking Fact  
**Attribute**: Booking Type

---

## Business Meaning

Booking Type classifies the nature of the booking transaction, distinguishing between new business acquisitions and renewal transactions. This classification is critical for analyzing new versus renewal revenue mix and customer retention.
