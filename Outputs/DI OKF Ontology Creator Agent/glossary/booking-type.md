# Booking Type

## Business Definition

Type of booking event, such as new business or renewal. This classification categorizes the nature of the booking transaction.

---

## Business Meaning

Booking Type classifies the booking transaction by its business purpose. Common booking types include new business (new customer acquisition), renewal (existing customer contract renewal), expansion (upsell to existing customer), and other transaction types. This classification enables analysis of business mix and growth sources.
