# Renewal Indicator

## Business Definition

Indicator showing whether the booking represents a renewal transaction.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.fact_bookings  
**Source Column**: is_renewal  
**Entity**: Booking Fact  
**Attribute**: Renewal Indicator

---

## Business Meaning

Renewal Indicator is a flag that identifies whether a booking transaction represents a renewal of an existing contract or subscription. This indicator supports renewal rate analysis and customer retention tracking.
