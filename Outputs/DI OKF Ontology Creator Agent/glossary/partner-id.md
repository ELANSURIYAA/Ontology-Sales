# Partner ID

## Business Definition

Business identifier assigned to the partner organization.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_partner  
**Source Column**: partner_id  
**Entity**: Partner Dimension  
**Attribute**: Partner ID

---

## Business Meaning

Partner ID is the business key used to identify partner organizations in operational systems. Unlike the Partner Key (surrogate key), the Partner ID is a meaningful business identifier that may be visible to users and partners.
