# Product Name

## Business Definition

Descriptive name of the product, service, or subscription offer.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_product

**Entity**: Product Dimension

## Business Meaning

Product Name is the descriptive name of the product or service used for reporting, analysis, and business communication. It provides human-readable identification of products in reports and dashboards.
