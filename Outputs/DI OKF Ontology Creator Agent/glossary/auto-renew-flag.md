# Auto Renew Flag

## Business Definition

Indicator showing whether the contract is configured to renew automatically.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_contract  
**Source Column**: auto_renew_flag  
**Entity**: Contract Dimension  
**Attribute**: Auto Renew Flag

---

## Business Meaning

The Auto Renew Flag indicates whether a contract is set to automatically renew at the end of its term. This attribute is critical for renewal forecasting, customer retention analysis, and proactive renewal management.
