# ACV USD

## Business Definition

Annualized contract value of the booking in US dollars. This represents the annual value of the contract for subscription and recurring revenue analysis.

---

## Business Meaning

ACV USD (Annual Contract Value) represents the annualized value of a booking transaction. For multi-year contracts, ACV normalizes the total contract value to an annual basis, enabling consistent comparison across contracts with different term lengths. This metric is critical for subscription business models and recurring revenue analysis.
