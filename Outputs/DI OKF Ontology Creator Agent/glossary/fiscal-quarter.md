# Fiscal Quarter

## Business Definition

Fiscal quarter used for period-based reporting and analysis. This represents the fiscal quarter within the fiscal year.

---

## Business Meaning

Fiscal Quarter represents the fiscal quarter within the fiscal year, typically designated as Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4. Each quarter spans three months and aligns with the organization's fiscal calendar. Fiscal quarters are critical for quarterly business reviews, performance tracking, and period-based planning. Quarter boundaries are determined by the fiscal year definition.
