# Fiscal Quarter

## Business Definition

Fiscal quarter associated with the date for reporting purposes.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date  
**Source Column**: fiscal_quarter  
**Entity**: Date Dimension  
**Attribute**: Fiscal Quarter

---

## Business Meaning

Fiscal Quarter represents the fiscal quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4) within the fiscal year for quarterly reporting and analysis. It enables quarter-level aggregation and quarter-over-quarter comparisons.
