# Region

## Business Definition

Broad global sales region associated with the booking, such as Americas, EMEA, or APJC.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_geography  
**Source Column**: region  
**Entity**: Geography Dimension  
**Attribute**: Region

---

## Business Meaning

Region represents the highest level of geographic hierarchy for sales reporting. It enables regional performance analysis and supports global sales management across major geographic markets.
