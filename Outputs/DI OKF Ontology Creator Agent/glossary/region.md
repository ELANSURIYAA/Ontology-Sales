# Region

## Business Definition

High-level geographic region used for sales reporting, such as Americas, EMEA, or APJC. This represents the broadest level of geographic classification.

---

## Business Meaning

Region represents the highest level of geographic classification used for sales territory management and reporting. Common regions include Americas (North and South America), EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa), APJC (Asia Pacific and Japan), and APAC (Asia Pacific). This classification enables high-level geographic performance tracking and regional strategy development.
