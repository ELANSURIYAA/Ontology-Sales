# Full Date

## Business Definition

Actual calendar date represented by the date record.

---

## Technical Mapping

**Source Table**: quotetobooking.dim_date  
**Source Column**: full_date  
**Entity**: Date Dimension  
**Attribute**: Full Date

---

## Business Meaning

Full Date is the actual calendar date value used for date-based filtering, sorting, and analysis. It represents the specific day when a booking transaction occurred.
