# Sales Representative Name

## Business Definition

Full name of the sales representative managing the account or opportunity.

## Business Meaning

Sales Representative Name is the descriptive name of the sales representative used for reporting, analysis, and business communication. It provides human-readable identification of sales representatives in reports and dashboards.
