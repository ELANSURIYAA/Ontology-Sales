# Technology Domain

## Business Definition

Technology area or solution domain to which the product belongs.

---

## Business Meaning

Technology Domain classifies products by their primary technology area or solution category. This classification enables analysis of technology portfolio performance, market trends, and strategic technology investments. Common domains include networking, security, collaboration, and observability.
