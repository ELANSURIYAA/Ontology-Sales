# Unit List Price USD

## Business Definition

Standard list price per unit in U.S. dollars before discounts are applied.

---

## Business Meaning

Unit List Price USD represents the standard published price for a single unit of product or service before any discounts, promotions, or negotiations. This baseline price is used to calculate discount amounts and measure price realization. All prices are recorded in U.S. dollars for consistent financial reporting.
