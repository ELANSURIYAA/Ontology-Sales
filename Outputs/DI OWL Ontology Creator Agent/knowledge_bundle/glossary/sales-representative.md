# Sales Representative

## Business Definition

Stores information about sales personnel responsible for managing customer relationships and booking transactions.

---

## Business Meaning

A sales representative is an individual responsible for managing customer accounts, developing opportunities, and closing sales transactions. Sales representatives are organized into teams, assigned to specific customer segments or territories, and accountable for achieving sales targets and quotas.
