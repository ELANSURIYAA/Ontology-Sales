# Product

## Business Definition

Stores descriptive information about products and offers sold to customers, including product family, technology domain, offer type, and business entity.

---

## Business Meaning

A product represents a sellable item, solution, or service offering provided to customers. Products are organized into families and technology domains, classified by offer type (hardware, software, SaaS), and managed by business entities. Product attributes enable portfolio analysis, technology domain performance tracking, and product strategy optimization.
