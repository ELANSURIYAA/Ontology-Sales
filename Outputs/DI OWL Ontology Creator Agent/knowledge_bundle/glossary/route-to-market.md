# Route to Market

## Business Definition

Describes the sales delivery path through which the product or service reached the customer.

---

## Business Meaning

Route to Market identifies the specific channel or path through which products and services are delivered to customers. Routes can include direct sales (company to customer), indirect sales through distributors or resellers, online channels, or hybrid models. Understanding route to market enables channel strategy optimization, resource allocation, and go-to-market effectiveness analysis.
