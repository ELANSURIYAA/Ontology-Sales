# Partner

## Business Definition

Stores information about channel and direct partners involved in the sales process, including partner type, partner tier, and route to market.

---

## Business Meaning

A partner represents an organization that collaborates in the sales process, either as a channel partner (distributor, reseller, systems integrator) or through direct sales engagement. Partners extend market reach, provide specialized expertise, and enable customer access across diverse markets and segments.
