# Partner

## Business Definition

Stores information about channel and direct partners involved in the sales process, including partner type, partner tier, and route to market.

---

## Business Meaning

A partner represents an organization that participates in the sales and distribution of products and services. Partners can include distributors, resellers, systems integrators, and direct sales channels. Partners extend market reach, provide specialized expertise, and enable scalable go-to-market strategies.
