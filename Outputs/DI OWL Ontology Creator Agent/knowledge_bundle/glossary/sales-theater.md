# Sales Theater

## Business Definition

Subregional sales area or theater used to organize market coverage and reporting.

---

## Business Meaning

Sales Theater represents a subregional division within a sales region. It provides a mid-level geographic grouping for sales operations, enabling more granular market analysis than regions while maintaining broader perspective than country-level reporting.
