# Customer

## Business Definition

Stores descriptive information about customers that place orders and generate bookings, including segment, industry, account tier, and headquarters location.

---

## Business Meaning

A customer represents an organization that purchases products and services, generating revenue through booking transactions. Customers are characterized by their business segment, industry vertical, strategic importance, and geographic location.
